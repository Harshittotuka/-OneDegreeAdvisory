01 What the CRM is

The One Degree CRM is the single place where every enquiry — a website form, a phone lead you add by hand, a spreadsheet import, or a course payment — becomes a lead you can track and act on. Each lead keeps its full story: who owns it, every status change, every comment, and eventually the complete student journey.

There are two kinds of user, and what you see depends on which one you are:

🧑‍💼 Counsellor

Works their own portfolio of assigned leads — calling, commenting, scheduling follow-ups and converting students. Sees only the leads assigned to them.

🛡️ Super admin

Sees every lead across the whole team, assigns owners, manages the team, and unlocks extra areas: newsletter subscribers, the audit log, and payment management.

In short

A lead is a person we might help. A student is a lead who enrolled. The CRM keeps both, with everything that happened in between.

02 Signing in

There are no passwords. You sign in with a one-time code (OTP) that is emailed to your registered address. Just enter your phone number or email, and we send you a fresh 6-digit code.

The sign-in flow, start to finish.

  1. Enter your phone or email. Either one works, as long as it is registered for you in the CRM.
  2. Check your email for the code. The code lands in your registered inbox and is valid for 5 minutes.
  3. Type the 6 digits. You get a few tries; after that, request a fresh code.
If you're stuck

The code is emailed, not texted. If it doesn't arrive, check spam, then ask a super admin to confirm the email on your account is correct. Only a super admin can add or fix team accounts.

04 Where leads come from

Leads arrive four ways, and they all land in the same list. However a lead arrives, it is saved with a unique reference number like OD-1042 so it's easy to find and quote.

Course paymentRazorpay checkout

Four sources, one shared pipeline.

🌐 From the website

When someone fills in a form on the public site — contact, profile evaluator, SOP, loan & accommodation, visa mock interview or the career library — a lead is created automatically and the exact answers are attached to it.

✍️ Added by hand

Use New lead to add someone from a call or referral. A name plus a phone or email is enough. Duplicates are blocked so the same person isn't added twice.

📄 Spreadsheet import

Upload a CSV file to add many leads at once. Rows without a valid phone or that already exist are skipped, and you get a count of what was added.

💳 Course payment

When someone pays for a program online, a lead is created (or matched to an existing one) and the payment is recorded against it. A successful payment turns the lead into an enrolled student.

Smart matching

If a website visitor or payer already exists as a lead — matched on phone or email — the new activity is added to their existing record instead of creating a duplicate. A soft-deleted lead is even brought back automatically.

06 Working a lead

Click any lead to open its workspace — a side panel with three tabs: Details, Timeline, and Student.

📞 Quick actions

Call, WhatsApp or email the lead in one tap. The WhatsApp option even pre-fills a friendly greeting with your name.

✏️ Details

Edit their name, contact, course & country of interest, priority, status and follow-up date. Super admins can also change the owner.

🕘 Timeline

The full history: your comments, automatic updates, website submissions and payments — filterable by type.

08 Follow-ups

A follow-up is a date and time you plan to next contact a lead. Set one from the Details tab, and it appears in the Follow-ups area and in your reminders.

  • Due today and overdue follow-ups are called out so nothing slips.
  • View them as a list or a calendar to plan your week.
  • When you've made the call, mark the follow-up complete — this also records that the lead was contacted just now.
Note

A lead holds one active follow-up at a time. Setting a new date replaces the old one, and completing it clears the slot — so schedule the next step as you close out the current one.

09 Becoming a student

When a lead decides to enrol, open their Student tab and choose Convert to Student. You record the student type, the starting stage, and any enrolment details (amount, date, payment reference, remarks).

Converting keeps the entire lead history intact and unlocks stage-by-stage tracking of their admission. A lead that pays online is converted to a student automatically.

Why a separate step

You can't reach "Enrolled student" just by changing the status — it always goes through Convert, so enrolment details are captured properly every time.

12 Newsletter subscribers Super admin

Every newsletter sign-up from the website is collected here with its source and status. Super admins can mark someone active or unsubscribed, remove entries, and export the list. Counsellors don't see this area.

13 Team management Super admin

Super admins add and manage the people who can use the CRM.

  • Add a member with a name, mobile, email and role (counsellor or super admin). They can sign in immediately with the OTP.
  • Edit their details, promote or demote between roles, and disable or restore access.
  • Remove a member — any leads they owned simply become unassigned so nothing is lost.
Safeguards

You can't change your own role or remove yourself, and at least one super admin must always remain active. A couple of "core" super admins are fixed in the server settings and can't be demoted or deleted from here — that's deliberate, so no one gets locked out.

14 Audit log Super admin

The audit log is a complete, searchable record of important actions across the CRM: sign-ins, lead creation and edits, conversions, follow-up completions, imports, and every team-management change. Each entry shows who did it, when, and — where relevant — what changed. Filter by action type, by person, or search the text. It's the go-to for "who did what, and when?".

15 PDF shortlisting tool

This tool builds a branded university-comparison sheet for a student. Upload the student's report PDF plus a shortlist spreadsheet, and it produces a polished PDF with a comparison table on the final page — ready to send. Available to everyone signed in.

  • The spreadsheet lists one university per column, with the compared attributes down the first column.
  • The sheet's name is used as the destination country on the table.
  • The student's name is picked up from the report automatically.

16 Good to know

📧 Your code comes by email

The login code is sent to your registered email, not by SMS. Keep that address current and reachable.

🧾 Import needs a CSV

The importer reads CSV files. If your data is in Excel, choose "Save As → CSV" first, then upload.

🗑️ Tidy up with "Junk"

Leads aren't deleted from the CRM. To clear out spam or test entries, set their status to Junk / invalid so they drop out of your working views.

🔁 No duplicates

The CRM matches people by phone and email, so the same person won't be added twice — new activity attaches to their existing record.

🎓 Enrol via the Student tab

"Enrolled student" is reached through Convert to Student — not by changing the status field.

🔒 You see your own leads

Counsellors work their assigned portfolio. Need a lead reassigned? Ask a super admin.

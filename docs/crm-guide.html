01 What the CRM is

The One Degree CRM is the single place where every enquiry — a website form, a phone lead you add by hand, a spreadsheet import, or a course payment — becomes a lead you can track and act on. Each lead keeps its full story: who owns it, every status change, every comment, and eventually the complete student journey.

There are two kinds of user, and what you see depends on which one you are:

🧑‍💼 Counsellor

Works their own portfolio of assigned leads — calling, commenting, scheduling follow-ups and converting students. Sees only the leads assigned to them.

🛡️ Super admin

Sees every lead across the whole team, assigns owners, manages the team, and unlocks extra areas: newsletter subscribers, the audit log, and payment management.

A third kind of account, Referral partner, is for partner companies: they see only the leads that name them as partner — read-only unless a super admin gives them edit access — and can open those students' journey planners read-only.

In short

A lead is a person we might help. A student is a lead who enrolled. The CRM keeps both, with everything that happened in between.

02 Signing in

There are no passwords. You sign in with a one-time code (OTP) that is emailed to your registered address. Just enter your phone number or email, and we send you a fresh 6-digit code.

The sign-in flow, start to finish.

  1. Enter your phone or email. Either one works, as long as it is registered for you in the CRM.
  2. Check your email for the code. The code lands in your registered inbox and is valid for 5 minutes.
  3. Type the 6 digits. You get a few tries; after that, request a fresh code.
  4. Keep me signed in. Ticked by default: this device stays signed in for 30 days, so you aren't asked for a code every visit. Untick it on a shared computer.
If you're stuck

The code is emailed, not texted. If it doesn't arrive, check spam, then ask a super admin to confirm the email on your account is correct. Only a super admin can add or fix team accounts.

04 Where leads come from

Leads arrive four ways, and they all land in the same list. However a lead arrives, it is saved with a unique reference number like OD-1042 so it's easy to find and quote.

Four sources, one shared pipeline.

🌐 From the website

When someone fills in a form on the public site — contact, profile evaluator, SOP, loan & accommodation, visa mock interview or the career library — a lead is created automatically and the exact answers are attached to it.

✍️ Added by hand

Use New lead to add someone from a call or referral. A name plus a phone or email is enough. Duplicates are blocked so the same person isn't added twice.

📄 Spreadsheet import

Upload a CSV file to add many leads at once. Rows without a valid phone or that already exist are skipped, and you get a count of what was added.

💳 Course payment

When someone pays for a program online, a lead is created (or matched to an existing one) and the payment is recorded against it. A successful payment turns the lead into an enrolled student.

Smart matching

If a website visitor or payer already exists as a lead — matched on phone or email — the new activity is added to their existing record instead of creating a duplicate. A soft-deleted lead is even brought back automatically.

08 Follow-ups

A follow-up is a date and time you plan to next contact a lead. Set one from the Details tab, and it appears in the Follow-ups area and in your reminders.

  • Due today and overdue follow-ups are called out so nothing slips.
  • View them as a list or a calendar to plan your week.
  • When you've made the call, mark the follow-up complete — this also records that the lead was contacted just now.
Note

A lead holds one active follow-up at a time. Setting a new date replaces the old one, and completing it clears the slot — so schedule the next step as you close out the current one.

09 Becoming a student

When a lead decides to enrol, open their Student tab and choose Convert to Student. You record the student type, the starting stage, and any enrolment details (amount, date, payment reference, remarks).

Converting keeps the entire lead history intact and unlocks stage-by-stage tracking of their admission. A lead that pays online is converted to a student automatically.

Why a separate step

You can't reach "Enrolled student" just by changing the status — it always goes through Convert, so enrolment details are captured properly every time.

12 Starting a planner & the student's login

  1. Open the student and go to the Student tab. The lead has to be an enrolled student with an email address — that email becomes their sign-in, and two students can't share one.
  2. Choose Start journey planner. The plan is built from ODA's template and the student's login is created.
  3. Copy the temporary password. It is shown once, at the top of the planner. Send it to the student with the sign-in link from the Student login page.
  4. The student signs in and picks their own password. From then on they see their plan in the student portal.
  5. Set the basics. Use Edit plan details on the Dashboard for the level, intake and focus.

Working a task

  • Include box (on the left): ticked means this student needs the task. Untick it and it greys out, drops out of every count, and the student no longer sees it.
  • Status: Not Started, In Progress, Submitted, Completed or Not Applicable. The student can change the tasks they own; Not Applicable is the counsellor's call.
  • Owner: a role (Student, Counsellor, University, Bank) or a person named on the Team page.
  • Target date, and — when you open the row — notes the student reads and the completion date.

➕ Your own tasks and stages

Add a task to this stage for something the template doesn't have, or Add a stage for a whole new one. What you add can be edited or removed; ODA's own tasks can only be switched off.

🔁 More than one of something

In Skill Enhancers, open an activity and choose Add another — a second internship, a third competition. Each is its own task, listed under the first, with its own status, owner and date.

✍️ Declaration form

The Student Declaration Form Sign-off has an Upload the signed form button. Uploading it moves the task to Submitted; the counsellor marks it Completed once checked.

🔎 Filters

Show one owner's tasks, or one status — "Still to do" hides everything finished.

14 Universities

  1. Add university. The university, country, programme, and fit — Reach, Match or Safe.
  2. Fill in the requirements (the Edit button at the top of the block). The tests and documents it asks for, its entry requirements, and the date applications close. The closing date appears in Deadlines and on the Calendar.
  3. Work the checklist. Five groups: Fit & requirements, Documents, Application, Interview & portfolio, Offer & visa. Switch on interview and portfolio only where the university asks for them.
  4. Record the offer. Unconditional, Conditional, Waitlist or Rejected — the Offer received task ticks itself.

15 Documents & essays

📎 Files

Upload a file: PDF, Word, JPG or PNG up to 10 MB, filed under a type (transcript, test score, passport, declaration form…) and, if it is for one university, that university. Files are private — only people signed in to the plan can open them.

📝 Essays

Write an essay for an SOP, personal statement or university essay. The student drafts and sends it for review; you approve it or send it back with feedback that shows on the essay.

History

Every document keeps a version number and a history of who changed what. The planner records drafts, reviews and approvals itself; use Log an edit for a change made outside it. The student sees the same history.

22 Partner codes Super admin

A partner code is a referral company plus a code we give them. Their code turns the Student Profiler into their link, and every profile submitted through it is tracked back to them.

  • Add a partner with their company name, email, custom code, phone, contact name and company link. The code becomes the link to hand out — /profiler?partner=CODE — and Copy link puts it on your clipboard.
  • What happens on a submission: the company is emailed a referral notice with the student's name, email, phone and headline preferences; our admissions mailbox gets its usual full report; and the lead is recorded in the CRM naming that partner code.
  • Watch the results from the Leads column on each row — it opens the leads list filtered to that code.
  • Pause a code to retire the link while keeping every lead it already brought in, or Delete the company outright (its leads stay, they just stop naming a code).
Good to know

Codes are matched whatever the capitals, so acme10 and ACME10 are the same link. A student who comes back through a different partner's link stays credited to whoever sent them first. And a code nobody issued — or one that's paused — changes nothing: the profile is still captured and we're still notified, it simply carries no partner. The partner's own notice never includes the full profile report; that stays internal.

23 Team management Super admin

Super admins add and manage the people who can use the CRM.

  • Add a member with a name, mobile, email and role (counsellor or super admin). They can sign in immediately with the OTP.
  • Edit their details, promote or demote between roles, and disable or restore access.
  • Remove a member — any leads they owned simply become unassigned so nothing is lost.
Safeguards

You can't change your own role or remove yourself, and at least one super admin must always remain active. A couple of "core" super admins are fixed in the server settings and can't be demoted or deleted from here — that's deliberate, so no one gets locked out.

24 Audit log Super admin

The audit log is a complete, searchable record of important actions across the CRM: sign-ins, lead creation and edits, conversions, follow-up completions, imports, and every team-management change. Each entry shows who did it, when, and — where relevant — what changed. Filter by action type, by person, or search the text. It's the go-to for "who did what, and when?".

25 PDF shortlisting tool

This tool builds a branded university-comparison sheet for a student. Upload the student's report PDF plus a shortlist spreadsheet, and it produces a polished PDF with a comparison table on the final page — ready to send. Available to everyone signed in.

  • The spreadsheet lists one university per column, with the compared attributes down the first column.
  • The sheet's name is used as the destination country on the table.
  • The student's name is picked up from the report automatically.

26 Mock interview links

The website's free visa mock interview runs a short round of 5 or 10 questions. A mock interview link you issue unlocks an extended round — 15, 20 or the full 39 questions — for one named student.

  1. Generate a new link on the Mock interviews page: the student's name and the question count, and if you like their email, mobile, destination country and a note.
  2. Copy the link and send it to the student. It works for 30 days unless you choose another expiry.
  3. Watch the results. Each link lists its attempts — when, how many questions were answered, and the score.
  4. Revoke a link to stop it working. You can revoke the links you issued; a super admin can revoke any.
Tip

Open the free page at the top shows exactly what the public round looks like, so you know what the student is comparing it with.

27 Blocked submissions Super admin

Every public form on the website carries a hidden field a person never sees. Bots fill it in; people don't. A submission that fills it is blocked: it never becomes a lead and no one is emailed — though the sender still sees the usual "thank you", so a bot has no sign it was caught. It is kept here instead, with which form it came from, what was sent, and the IP address, so a super admin can spot repeat offenders and check nothing genuine was caught.

If a real enquiry is here

Add the person by hand with New lead — blocked submissions are a record, not a queue, and are not turned into leads from this page.

29 Good to know

📧 Your code comes by email

The login code is sent to your registered email, not by SMS. Keep that address current and reachable.

🧾 Import needs a CSV

The importer reads CSV files. If your data is in Excel, choose "Save As → CSV" first, then upload.

🗑️ Tidy up with "Junk"

Leads aren't deleted from the CRM. To clear out spam or test entries, set their status to Junk / invalid so they drop out of your working views.

🔁 No duplicates

The CRM matches people by phone and email, so the same person won't be added twice — new activity attaches to their existing record.

🎓 Enrol via the Student tab

"Enrolled student" is reached through Convert to Student — not by changing the status field.

🔒 You see your own leads

Counsellors work their assigned portfolio. Need a lead reassigned? Ask a super admin.
